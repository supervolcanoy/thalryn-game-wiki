## Detection Profile

- Uses elevated **sightlines** from above.
- Most dangerous in clearings and exposed ridges.

## Behavior

Shadow Eagles perform fast pass attacks from altitude, forcing the player to reposition under cover.

## Counterplay

- Move through dense canopy whenever possible.
- Use terrain overhangs and cliff shadows.
- Avoid long stationary actions in open biomes.
