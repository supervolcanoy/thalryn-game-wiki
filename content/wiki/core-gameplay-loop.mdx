## Loop Structure

Thalryn follows a repeated **survive-explore-craft-advance** cycle:

1. **Dawn:** scout and route-plan.
2. **Day:** gather materials, craft, and push outward.
3. **Dusk:** return or fortify before night.
4. **Night:** survive high-pressure creature activity.
5. Repeat at greater distance and risk.

## Exploration

- Infinite procedural traversal with no hard map boundaries.
- Navigation depends on landmarks and memory.
- Deep underground and secret biome discovery are major progression spikes.

## Resource Gathering

Core resources include:

- wood, stone, slate (early-mid tiers),
- warped slate and crystals (advanced),
- molten soul and oil crystals (late progression),
- obsidian fragments (ultra-rare biome progression).

## Crafting Philosophy

- Basic crafting has low setup friction.
- Advanced/legendary crafting depends on biome depth and danger.
- Knowledge progression matters as much as item progression.

## Combat and Stealth

Combat exists but is intentionally punishing.  
Best outcomes usually come from:

- route control,
- stealth discipline,
- environmental advantage,
- and selective engagement.
