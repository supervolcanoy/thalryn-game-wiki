## Detection Profile

- Triggered by close-range **proximity** near molten soul pools.
- Sustained threat through attach-and-drain behavior.

## Behavior

Soul Leeches turn resource collection zones into attrition traps. They pressure both movement and healing economy.

## Counterplay

- Scan pool perimeters before harvesting.
- Remove attached leeches immediately.
- Avoid prolonged stationary actions near molten pools.
