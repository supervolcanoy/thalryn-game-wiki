## Character Concept

The player character is an ordinary **Animal Control Officer** stranded in Thalryn's cursed ecosystem.

## Attribute Model

- Health
- Stamina
- Hunger
- Thirst
- Optional sanity (design-dependent)

## Gameplay Implication

Thalryn's progression is not hero scaling. The character remains grounded while player mastery grows through systems knowledge, preparation, and risk management.

## Inventory

Suggested baseline from the game specification:

- ~20 slot inventory
- 4 to 6 quick-access hotbar slots
