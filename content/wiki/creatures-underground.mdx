## Zone Threat Model

Underground encounters emphasize constrained visibility, ambush angles, and environmental attrition.

## Known Threat Types

### Cave Crawler

- Group swarm threat.
- Individually weak, collectively dangerous.
- Full profile: [Cave Crawler](/wiki/cave-crawler)

### Soul Leech

- Proximity-based attach threat near molten soul pools.
- Sustained drain pressure if not removed quickly.
- Full profile: [Soul Leech](/wiki/soul-leech)

### Deep Stalker

- High danger stalker profile.
- Excels in dark corridors and abandoned mine routes.
- Full profile: [Deep Stalker](/wiki/deep-stalker)

## Tactical Implications

- Light discipline is critical.
- Vertical spacing and route memory matter.
- Resource greed is heavily punished in this layer.
