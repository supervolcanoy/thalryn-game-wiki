## Win Condition

Primary victory path is **escape**:

- locate the abandoned car,
- gather oil crystals from Deep Undergrounds,
- refuel,
- and drive out of the forest.

## Loss Condition

- Health reaching zero ends the run.
- Thalryn is designed around **permadeath**.
- Auto-save slot is deleted on death.

## Optional Endgame Route

Defeating **The Hazard** is optional for victory, but yields the strongest rewards.

## Difficulty Scaling

Difficulty scales naturally by:

- distance from spawn,
- biome depth,
- and time phase (night multiplier).

## Day/Night Rule Impact

Day supports productive expansion.  
Night sharply increases threat density and encounter pressure.
