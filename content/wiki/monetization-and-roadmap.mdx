## Monetization Model

Thalryn is specified as a **premium buy-to-play** title with no microtransaction dependency in the base model.

## Post-Launch Opportunities

- paid biome/creature expansions,
- free quality-of-life and balancing updates,
- potential co-op expansion as a major feature release.

## Pricing Direction

Current planning range in the specification sits in the mid-tier premium bracket.
