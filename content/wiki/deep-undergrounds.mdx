## Zone Summary

The Deep Undergrounds are a separate gameplay layer below the surface forest. They are darker, denser, and more lethal than surface traversal.

## Notable Features

- Underground lakes
- Crystal caverns
- Abandoned mineshafts
- Molten soul pools
- Oil crystal veins

## Progression Relevance

This zone is the source of core progression resources:

- **Oil Crystals** for escape vehicle refueling
- **Molten Soul** for Ultra-Bomb crafting
- Late-tier materials for advanced crafting chains
