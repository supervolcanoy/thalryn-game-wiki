## Crafting Role

The Ultra-Bomb is the only documented tool capable of forcing The Hazard into the internal core phase.

## Recipe Chain

- Craft and fill **4 buckets of molten soul**
- Combine them into Ultra-Bomb

## Material Source

Molten soul is found in deep underground pools and is dangerous to harvest without proper preparation.

## Tactical Use

Detonate during **Phase 5** of The Hazard fight. Using it at the wrong time risks wasting the run's most expensive item.
