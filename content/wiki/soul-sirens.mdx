## Description

Soul Sirens are tall obsidian monoliths with purple-veined glow and a low hum audible from roughly 50 meters away.

## Mechanics

- Activation causes a brief player stun.
- Nearby enemies are attracted to the Siren location.
- Activations are tracked globally during a run.
- The fifth total activation summons **The Hazard**.

## Location

Soul Sirens appear primarily in **Obsidian City**, with rare surface spawns.

## Strategy Notes

Most runs treat Siren activation as a planned sequence:

1. Prepare for combat and escape.
2. Avoid accidental fifth activation.
3. Craft the Ultra-Bomb before final trigger.
