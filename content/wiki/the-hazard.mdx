## Overview

The Hazard is the optional world boss of **Thalryn**. It has **10,000 HP**, appears only after the player activates five Soul Sirens, and represents the highest-risk encounter in the game.

## Trigger and Spawn

- Trigger condition: **5 total Soul Siren activations**
- Spawn context: at the base of major cliff formations
- Spawn effects: brief whiteout, distorted scream, and terrain cracking with molten soul flooding low zones

## Combat Phases

1. **Phase 1 (10,000-8,000)**: sweeping limb strikes and early arena pressure.
2. **Phase 2 (8,000-6,000)**: shockwave ground-pounds and minion spawns.
3. **Phase 3 (6,000-4,000)**: disorienting scream attacks and faster aggression.
4. **Phase 4 (4,000-2,000)**: terrain consumption and heavier environmental hazards.
5. **Phase 5 (2,000-0)**: critical Ultra-Bomb detonation window.
6. **Phase 6 (internal)**: the player is drawn into the body to destroy the core.

## Defeat Condition

The intended strategy is to detonate the **Ultra-Bomb** during Phase 5, then complete the internal phase by destroying the exposed heart/core.

## Rewards

- **Soul Sword**
- **Soul Arrows**
- **Soul Shard**
