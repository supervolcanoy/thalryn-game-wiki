## Visual Direction

Thalryn aims for oppressive realism:

- muted/desaturated palette,
- strong darkness contrast,
- biologically plausible creature corruption,
- and minimal stylization that would break grounded tone.

## Lighting Principles

- Day: filtered forest light and readability.
- Night: severe visibility constraints.
- Underground: darkness-first with selective local sources.

## Audio Pillars

Audio functions as a survival signal network:

- directional creature signatures,
- ambient state shifts by time phase,
- hazard escalation stingers,
- and sparse music to preserve tension headroom.

## Music Usage

Music is intentionally restrained and event-biased rather than constantly present.
