## Detection Profile

- Primarily reacts to **scent** and **sound**.
- High danger once inside close-range engagement.

## Behavior

Corrupted Bears control local territory and punish direct confrontation. They are among the deadliest non-boss surface encounters.

## Counterplay

- Prioritize stealth routes and scent management.
- Avoid greedy looting in bear-controlled zones.
- Use hard terrain breaks before repositioning.
