## Concept Summary

**Thalryn** is an infinite, procedurally generated open-world survival horror game set in a cursed forest ecosystem.  
The player is an **Animal Control Officer** stranded in a hostile world with no scripted narrative to guide them.

The experience is driven by:

- emergent survival decisions,
- environmental storytelling,
- systemic creature behavior,
- and escalating risk tied to exploration.

## Genre

Open-World Survival Horror with:

- exploration,
- crafting,
- resource management,
- stealth-first encounters,
- and high-stakes optional boss progression.

## Target Audience

Players who enjoy tense, systems-driven survival horror, especially fans of:

- **Subnautica**
- **The Forest**
- **Darkwood**

## Unique Selling Points

- Truly infinite procedural world with chunk streaming.
- No hard narrative rails; lore is environmental and fragmentary.
- Physical, biological horror tone (no glitch aesthetic).
- Player-triggered world-boss path via Soul Sirens.
- Multi-layer world structure: surface, deep undergrounds, ultra-rare secret biome.
- Strong day/night asymmetry that changes risk profile and strategy.
