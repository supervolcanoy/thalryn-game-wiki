## Generation Model

Thalryn’s world is generated continuously via chunk streaming with no hard horizontal border.

## Surface Biome Set

Core biome classes include:

- Dense Forest
- Dead Forest
- Swamp/Wetland
- Rocky Highlands
- Clearings/Meadows
- Ruins/Structures
- Obsidian City (ultra-rare)

## Navigation Philosophy

Players are intended to feel disoriented.  
No default minimap means route memory, landmarks, and handcrafted markers become critical survival tools.

## Risk Geography

Distance and biome rarity correlate with stronger reward potential and harsher threat profiles.
