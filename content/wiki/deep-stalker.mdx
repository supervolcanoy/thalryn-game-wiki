## Detection Profile

- Reacts strongly to **sound** and can engage effectively in darkness.
- Rapid pursuit when alerted.

## Behavior

Deep Stalkers patrol abandoned mineshafts and pressure players through sudden acceleration and tight-space aggression.

## Counterplay

- Keep noise low in shafts and tunnel intersections.
- Pre-plan retreat routes before entering patrol zones.
- Prioritize line breaks and elevation shifts when escaping.
