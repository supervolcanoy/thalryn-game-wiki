## Progression Philosophy

Thalryn progression is not level-gated.  
The player grows stronger mainly through:

- understanding systems,
- discovering risk-efficient routes,
- and acquiring better tools through dangerous exploration.

## Primary Progression Axes

- Crafting tier advancement
- Gear quality escalation
- Biome-depth exploration capability
- Optional Hazard encounter chain

## Hazard Path as Endgame Arc

1. Locate/activate Sirens.
2. Prepare molten soul logistics.
3. Craft Ultra-Bomb.
4. Execute phase-accurate boss progression.
5. Claim top-tier rewards.
