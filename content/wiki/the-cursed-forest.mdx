## Core World Model

Thalryn uses an infinite procedural surface world with chunk-based streaming and no hard boundaries.

## Surface Biomes

Representative biome set from the game specification:

- Dense Forest
- Dead Forest
- Swamp / Wetland
- Rocky Highlands
- Clearings / Meadows
- Ruins / Structures
- Obsidian City (ultra-rare)

## Day/Night Risk Split

A full cycle is approximately 24 real-time minutes. Day supports exploration and crafting; night sharply increases creature danger and pressure.

## Navigation Philosophy

No minimap by default. Orientation relies on landmarks, celestial cues, and player-made trail systems.
