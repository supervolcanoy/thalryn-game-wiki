## Spawn Rarity

Obsidian City is an ultra-rare biome with about **0.01%** generation chance per chunk cluster.

## Biome Identity

The zone is defined by towering black architecture, ruined plazas, and concentrated danger. It is one of the most important late-game discovery points in Thalryn.

## Key Locations

- Temple of Arackniss
- Obsidian Glade
- Crypt of Whispers
- Collapsed Gates
- Sunken Plazas

## Why It Matters

- Dense Soul Siren presence
- Unique creature threats
- Access to rare materials and lore-rich structures
