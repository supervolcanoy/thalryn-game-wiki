## Detection Profile

- Primarily reacts to **sound** and **sight**.
- Uses quick direction changes to punish noisy movement.

## Behavior

Mutated Fox encounters are commonly pair-based. One fox pressures directly while the other attempts a lateral flank.

## Counterplay

- Keep line of sight breaks between trees and rock.
- Reduce sprinting and branch-breaking when nearby.
- Avoid tunnel vision in close-range chases.
