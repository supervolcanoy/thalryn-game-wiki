## Crafting Tiers

### Basic

Early survival essentials like torches and simple tools.

### Intermediate

Improved tools and practical utility items, including the **bucket**.

### Advanced

Reinforced tools and tactical utilities that support deeper runs.

### Legendary

Endgame-grade items tied to hazardous resource chains, especially the **Ultra-Bomb** path.

## Key Materials

- **Wood / Stone:** reliable early economy.
- **Slate / Warped Slate:** tool and progression bridge.
- **Glowing Crystals:** advanced crafting path support.
- **Molten Soul:** dangerous late-game ingredient.
- **Oil Crystals:** required for escape win condition.
- **Obsidian Fragments:** ultra-rare biome-linked crafting value.

## Critical Recipe Chain

1. Craft **Bucket**.
2. Harvest **Molten Soul** from deep pools.
3. Fill four buckets.
4. Fuse into **Ultra-Bomb**.
5. Use in The Hazard encounter during the correct phase window.
