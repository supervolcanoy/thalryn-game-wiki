## Terms

### Chunk

A discrete unit of generated world terrain loaded/unloaded around the player.

### Deep Undergrounds

The subterranean gameplay layer containing unique resources and threat ecology.

### Molten Soul

A hazardous purple liquid used in endgame crafting chains.

### Obsidian City

Ultra-rare secret biome containing high-value risk/reward systems.

### Oil Crystals

Resource required for escape vehicle refueling in the primary win path.

### Permadeath

Run-ending death model where the world/save is erased.

### Soul Siren

Progression monolith tied to the global activation counter and Hazard trigger.

### The Hazard

Optional world boss summoned after five Siren activations.

### Ultra-Bomb

Legendary crafted item required for correct boss progression execution.
