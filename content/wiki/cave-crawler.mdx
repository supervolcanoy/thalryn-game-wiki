## Detection Profile

- Tracks vibration and local movement.
- Individually weak but dangerous in packs.

## Behavior

Cave Crawlers usually appear in groups of 5 to 10 and overwhelm players who overcommit to narrow corridors.

## Counterplay

- Maintain spacing and avoid dead-end pathing.
- Clear swarm edges before pushing deeper.
- Use movement discipline to avoid chain pulls.
