## Overview

Surface creatures in Thalryn are designed as mutated but physically plausible organisms.  
Threat expression varies by **sight**, **sound**, and **scent** sensitivity.

## Notable Surface Threats

### Mutated Fox

- Pair-based pressure behavior.
- Fast and disruptive at night.
- Full profile: [Mutated Fox](/wiki/mutated-fox)

### Blind Bloodhound

- Scent-tracking specialist.
- Water crossings and scent disruption are core counters.
- Full profile: [Blind Bloodhound](/wiki/blind-bloodhound)

### Shadow Eagle

- Aerial pressure in open spaces.
- Tree cover is key defensive terrain.
- Full profile: [Shadow Eagle](/wiki/shadow-eagle)

### Aberrant Deer

- Erratic engagement pattern.
- Can rapidly shift from passive to high-threat charge behavior.
- Full profile: [Aberrant Deer](/wiki/aberrant-deer)

### Corrupted Bear

- Apex surface threat profile.
- Extremely dangerous in direct melee exchanges.
- Full profile: [Corrupted Bear](/wiki/corrupted-bear)
