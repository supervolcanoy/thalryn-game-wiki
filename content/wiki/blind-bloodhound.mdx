## Detection Profile

- Tracks by **scent** rather than sight.
- Long pursuit endurance makes it dangerous in extended runs.

## Behavior

Blind Bloodhounds are slower than many predators but difficult to shake once they acquire a trail.

## Counterplay

- Cross water to break scent continuity.
- Use mud/scent disruption mechanics when available.
- Avoid repeated backtracking through your own trail lines.
