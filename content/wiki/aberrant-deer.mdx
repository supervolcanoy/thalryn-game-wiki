## Detection Profile

- Responds to **sound** and **sight**.
- Appears non-hostile until provoked or triggered.

## Behavior

Aberrant Deer are deceptive threats. Their movement can appear harmless before a sudden aggressive charge sequence.

## Counterplay

- Keep distance even during passive animation states.
- Avoid loud movement in close proximity.
- Treat narrow paths as high-risk if deer are nearby.
